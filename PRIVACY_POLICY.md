# Privacy Policy — Trojan Technologies WhatsApp Assistant

_Last updated: August 2, 2026_

Trojan Technologies ("we", "us", "our") operates an automated WhatsApp assistant to help customers in Qatar with inquiries about our printer and copier sales and service offerings. This policy explains what information we collect through that assistant, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you message our WhatsApp number, we collect and store:

- Your WhatsApp phone number.
- Your name and company name, if you provide them or they are on file from a prior business relationship.
- The content of your messages and our replies, including message timestamps.
- A summary of your product or service interest, if you inquire about a purchase or contract, so our sales team can follow up.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

- Respond to your questions about our products, services, and pricing.
- Connect you with our sales team when you express interest in a purchase or service contract.
- Maintain a record of past conversations so we can provide continuity of service if you contact us again.
- Improve the accuracy and quality of our automated responses.

## 3. Automated Processing and Third-Party Service Providers

Your messages are processed by the following third parties on our behalf:

- **Meta Platforms, Inc.** — provides the WhatsApp Business Platform used to send and receive messages. Meta's handling of your data is also governed by [WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **A third-party AI language model provider** — the text of your messages is sent to an AI service to generate the assistant's replies. This provider processes message content solely to produce a response and does not use it for any purpose of their own outside providing this service to us.
- **Database and hosting providers** — used to store conversation records securely.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

## 4. Human Review

Our sales team may review conversation summaries and message history when following up on an inquiry you've raised, or when a team member takes over a conversation directly.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your conversation history and contact details for as long as necessary to maintain our business relationship with you and provide ongoing support, or until you request deletion as described below.

## 6. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of your information from our records.
- Stop receiving messages from us — simply tell us and we will not initiate further contact, subject to any messages required to complete a transaction already in progress.

## 7. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our WhatsApp assistant is intended for business inquiries from adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
